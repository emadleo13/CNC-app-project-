## Facebook — English

```
Mazak alarm EX1012 — OVERTRAVEL: - DIRECTION

An axis has reached or would exceed its negative software stroke limit. Motion in the negative direction is blocked.

Most common causes:
• Program Z depth exceeds the negative travel limit
• Tool length offset not active, causing Z to travel further than expected
• Work offset Z value set near the machine's mechanical limit

First thing to try: Press RESET, then jog the axis in the positive direction away from the limit
```
