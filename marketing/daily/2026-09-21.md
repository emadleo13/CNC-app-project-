## LinkedIn — English

```
G99 — Return to R Plane (Canned Cycles)

After a canned cycle, G99 retracts Z only to the R plane instead of the full clearance height, which is why programmers reach for it on any part with a long run of repeated holes: it takes real seconds out of every cycle.

— After canned cycle, retract Z only to R plane. Faster for repeated holes.
— Ensure clearance between holes when using G99 with fixtures.

The saving only holds if that R plane actually clears everything bolted to the table. A clamp, a riser, or a fixture wall sitting inside the shortened retract path turns a time-saver into a collision, and re-fixturing a scrapped part costs a lot more than G99 ever gave back. Confirm it once at setup, not on the first hole.
```
