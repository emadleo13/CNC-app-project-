Axis synchronization has been lost during gantry operation. The position difference between coupled axes exceeds the allowed tolerance.

Most common causes:
• Mechanical binding on one axis of the gantry pair
• Encoder fault on one axis causing position mismatch
• Different mechanical load on gantry axes

First thing to try: Check both gantry axes for mechanical binding or obstruction
