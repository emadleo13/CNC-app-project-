Ever scrolled through someone else's probing macro and hit a line you didn't recognize? For a lot of Haas programmers, that line is M96.

M96 — Jump If Probe Contact

Conditional branch if skip signal (probe) is active.

Syntax:  M96 P_ Q_

⚠ P=block number, Q=comparison value.

Get either one wrong and nothing alarms — the branch just doesn't fire, and the check the macro was supposed to make quietly gets skipped.
