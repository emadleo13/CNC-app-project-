Heidenhain alarm 10 — AXIS LIMIT SWITCH ACTIVATED

One or more axes have triggered a hardware or software travel limit switch. All axis motion is stopped.

Most common causes:
• Programmed move exceeds the machine travel limits
• Axis was jogged past a limit switch in manual mode
• Software limit switch values are set incorrectly

First thing to try: In manual mode, jog the axis away from the limit switch in the opposite direction
