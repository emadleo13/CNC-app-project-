## 3. Answering an alarm question in a group or on Reddit

This is the highest-converting thing you can do, and the only self-promotion most communities
tolerate. **Answer completely first.** The link is a footnote, never the point.

```
That's <BRAND> alarm <CODE> — <TITLE>.

It usually comes down to one of these:
1. <cause>
2. <cause>
3. <cause>

Start with <first fix>. If that doesn't clear it, <second fix>.

(I keep a write-up of this one here if you want the long version: <alarm page URL>)
```

**Rules that keep you from getting banned**
- Never post a bare link. Ever.
- Never post the same text twice — moderators and the algorithm both catch it.
- Reply to replies. One answered follow-up beats ten new posts.
- On Reddit, read the subreddit rules first. r/CNC and r/Machinists remove self-promo on sight,
  but a genuinely useful answer with a relevant link at the end is normally fine.

## 7. Objection responses

**"Is it really free?"**
> The calculators, the G-code analyzer and the full alarm/code reference are free and always will
> be. There are no ads. Only some of the AI features need a subscription — that's what pays the
> server bill.

**"Why do you need an account?"**
> You don't. It signs you in anonymously so your saved calculations survive an app update. No
> email, no password.

**"Can I trust the cutting data?"**
> It's a starting point from published material data, not gospel. Always verify against your own
> machine, tooling and material — the app says so on every result.

**"Does it work offline?"**
> The calculators and the whole alarm and G-code reference, yes. Only the AI answers need a
> connection.

## 8. What NOT to do

- Do not buy installs or run generic "app install" ads — Play's ranking punishes installs that
  don't open, and CNC operators are far too narrow an audience for broad targeting to be worth it.
- Do not post the same message in 15 groups on the same day. That is exactly what gets accounts
  flagged.
- Do not ask for 5 stars. Ask for honest feedback; the in-app prompt handles the rest.
- Do not add ads to the app to monetise. "No ads" is currently one of your strongest lines.
