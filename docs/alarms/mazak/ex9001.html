Mazak Alarm EX9001 — HYDRAULIC PRESSURE LOW

critical

The hydraulic unit pressure (used for chucking, tailstock, ATC, or pallet clamping depending on machine model) has dropped below the minimum threshold.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX9001

  • Hydraulic oil level low
  • Hydraulic pump motor fault or thermal overload tripped
  • External leak in hydraulic lines or fittings
  • Pressure switch or gauge fault giving a false reading

How to clear alarm EX9001

  1. Check the hydraulic tank oil level and top up with the specified hydraulic oil
  2. Inspect hoses and fittings for visible leaks
  3. Check the hydraulic pump motor overload relay and reset if tripped
  4. Verify actual pressure with a calibrated gauge before replacing the pressure switch
  5. Do not run chucking/clamping operations with low hydraulic pressure — workholding may release
