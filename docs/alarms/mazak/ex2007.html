Mazak Alarm EX2007 — SPINDLE ORIENTATION ERROR

error

The spindle (C-axis / Spindle Orientation, M19) failed to reach the commanded angular position within the allowed time.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX2007

  • Spindle position encoder fault or contamination
  • Spindle brake not releasing correctly
  • Orientation timeout too short for a heavy tool/chuck assembly
  • Belt slippage on belt-driven spindle models

How to clear alarm EX2007

  1. Check the spindle encoder cable and connector for coolant contamination
  2. Verify the spindle brake releases during the orientation cycle
  3. Increase the orientation timeout parameter if heavy tooling is used
  4. Inspect drive belt condition and tension on belt-driven spindles
  5. Retry M19 after clearing the alarm — intermittent encoder noise may clear
