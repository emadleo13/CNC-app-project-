Mazak Alarm EX9101 — LUBRICATION ALARM: OIL LEVEL LOW

error

The centralized way/ballscrew lubrication system oil reservoir is low or the lubrication pump did not build pressure within the expected cycle time.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX9101

  • Lube oil reservoir empty or near empty
  • Lubrication pump motor or solenoid fault
  • Air trapped in the lubrication lines
  • Lube line blocked or disconnected at a distribution block

How to clear alarm EX9101

  1. Refill the lubrication reservoir with the oil grade specified in the maintenance manual
  2. Manually cycle the lube pump (purge function) to clear trapped air
  3. Inspect distribution lines and metering blocks for blockages or leaks
  4. Do not continue running — inadequate lubrication causes rapid wear of guides and ballscrews
