Mazak Alarm EX4801 — ATC ALARM: TOOL CHANGE ARM NOT IN POSITION

critical

The automatic tool changer arm did not reach its expected position during a tool change (M06) sequence.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX4801

  • ATC arm mechanically jammed by debris or an oversized tool
  • ATC arm position sensor misaligned or failed
  • Hydraulic or pneumatic pressure too low to complete the arm stroke
  • Tool not properly seated/clamped before the change began

How to clear alarm EX4801

  1. Do NOT force the ATC arm by hand — follow the Mazak ATC recovery procedure in maintenance mode
  2. Check air/hydraulic pressure gauges against the specified minimum
  3. Inspect the ATC arm and magazine area for chips, coolant residue, or obstructions
  4. Verify ATC sensor signals on the I/O diagnostic screen
  5. Confirm the tool was fully seated in the spindle before the change started
