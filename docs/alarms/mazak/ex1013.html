Mazak Alarm EX1013 — SERVO ALARM: EXCESS ERROR (FOLLOWING ERROR)

critical

The difference between the commanded and actual axis position (following error) exceeded the allowable tolerance.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX1013

  • Mechanical binding, collision, or obstruction on the axis
  • Worn or damaged ballscrew, linear guide, or coupling
  • Servo amplifier fault or drive parameter mismatch
  • Programmed feedrate or acceleration exceeds axis capability

How to clear alarm EX1013

  1. Power off and check the axis for binding by moving it manually (where possible)
  2. Inspect the ballscrew, guides, and motor coupling for wear or looseness
  3. Check servo amplifier alarm LEDs for a secondary drive code
  4. Reduce rapid traverse override and retest before resuming full speed
  5. Contact Mazak service if the alarm persists with no mechanical cause
