Mazak Alarm EX1014 — SERVO READY OFF

critical

A servo amplifier has dropped its READY signal. The NC cannot enable axis motion until the drive reports ready.

Likely causes of Mazak alarm EX1014

  • Drive power supply fault or undervoltage
  • Emergency stop or safety chain interrupted drive enable
  • Drive overheating or internal fault
  • DRIVE-CLiQ / servo communication cable fault (M800/M80 series)

How to clear alarm EX1014

  1. Check that all E-STOP buttons are released and safety doors closed
  2. Power cycle the control (main breaker off, wait 30 seconds, then on)
  3. Inspect servo amplifier status LEDs for fault codes
  4. Check servo communication cabling for damage or loose connectors
