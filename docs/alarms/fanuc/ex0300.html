Fanuc Alarm EX0300 — APC ALARM

error

The automatic pallet changer has encountered a fault condition. The pallet exchange sequence could not complete.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm EX0300

  • Pallet not properly seated on locating pins
  • Pallet clamp not fully engaged
  • APC mechanism jammed or obstructed
  • Hydraulic pressure insufficient for pallet clamping
  • APC position sensor fault

How to clear alarm EX0300

  1. Check pallet seating and clamp status indicators
  2. Verify hydraulic pressure is at operating level
  3. Inspect APC mechanism for obstructions
  4. Check APC sensor signals in PMC diagnostics
  5. Follow machine-specific APC recovery procedure
