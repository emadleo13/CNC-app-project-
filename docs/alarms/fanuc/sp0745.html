Fanuc Alarm SP0745 — SPINDLE MOTOR OVERHEAT

error

The spindle motor temperature has exceeded its rated maximum.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SP0745

  • Extended heavy cutting without adequate cool-down
  • Spindle motor cooling fan failure
  • Blocked motor cooling passages
  • Motor bearing failure increasing friction load

How to clear alarm SP0745

  1. Stop spindle and allow to cool for 20-30 minutes
  2. Check spindle motor blower/fan is running
  3. Clear obstructions from motor cooling passages
  4. Reduce spindle load and duty cycle
  5. Inspect spindle bearings if problem persists
