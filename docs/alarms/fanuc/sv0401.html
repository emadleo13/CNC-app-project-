Fanuc Alarm SV0401 — SERVO ALARM: AXIS VRDY OFF

critical

The servo drive for the affected axis lost its ready signal (VRDY). The drive is not ready to accept commands.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0401

  • Servo drive powered off or main circuit breaker tripped
  • Drive fault or internal drive error
  • Weak or faulty power supply to servo drive
  • FSSB (Fiber optic servo bus) communication error

How to clear alarm SV0401

  1. Check servo drive power supply and circuit breakers
  2. Look at servo amplifier display for specific error code
  3. Verify FSSB cable connections between CNC and amplifiers
  4. Check servo amplifier LED indicators for fault codes
  5. Power cycle the machine completely
