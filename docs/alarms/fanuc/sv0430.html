Fanuc Alarm SV0430 — SERVO ALARM: MOTOR OVERHEAT

error

The servo motor temperature sensor detected the motor is overheating.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0430

  • Sustained heavy cutting load
  • Motor cooling fan failure
  • Blocked motor ventilation
  • Motor bearing failure causing friction
  • Ambient temperature too high

How to clear alarm SV0430

  1. Stop cutting and allow motor to cool (15-30 minutes)
  2. Check motor cooling fan operation
  3. Clear any obstruction from motor ventilation
  4. Reduce cutting load and duty cycle
  5. If alarm recurs quickly, check motor bearings for noise/heat
