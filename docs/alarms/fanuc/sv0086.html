Fanuc Alarm SV0086 — DR OFF SIGNAL

critical

The servo ready (DR) signal is OFF. One or more servo drives are not reporting a ready condition to the CNC.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0086

  • Servo amplifier not powered on
  • Servo amplifier internal fault
  • Emergency stop circuit open
  • Servo power supply circuit breaker tripped
  • FSSB communication lost between CNC and amplifier

How to clear alarm SV0086

  1. Check servo amplifier power supply and circuit breakers
  2. Verify E-Stop chain is satisfied and safety relay is energized
  3. Check servo amplifier display/LEDs for fault codes
  4. Inspect FSSB fiber optic cables between CNC and amplifiers
  5. Power cycle the machine after resolving the root cause
