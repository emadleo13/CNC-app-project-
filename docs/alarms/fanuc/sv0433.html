Fanuc Alarm SV0433 — SERVO ALARM: CONVERTER HIGH VOLTAGE

error

The DC bus voltage exceeded the maximum safe level, usually during deceleration (regenerative braking).

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0433

  • Regenerative energy exceeds resistor capacity
  • Regeneration resistor open circuit or failed
  • Very rapid deceleration of heavy load
  • Regeneration resistor undersized for application

How to clear alarm SV0433

  1. Check regenerative braking resistor is connected and not open circuit
  2. Measure resistor value — compare to specification
  3. Reduce deceleration rates if possible
  4. If resistor is good, check amplifier regeneration circuit
