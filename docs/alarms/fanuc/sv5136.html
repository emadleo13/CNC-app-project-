Fanuc Alarm SV5136 — ABSOLUTE PULSE CODER: COMMUNICATION ERROR

critical

Communication between the CNC and an absolute-position pulse coder (encoder) was interrupted or corrupted.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV5136

  • Encoder feedback cable disconnected, damaged, or coolant-contaminated
  • Encoder battery completely discharged, corrupting absolute data
  • Electrical noise on the feedback cable
  • Pulse coder hardware failure

How to clear alarm SV5136

  1. Check the feedback cable connections at the motor and amplifier
  2. Replace the encoder backup battery and re-establish absolute position if data was lost
  3. Inspect cable shielding and routing away from power cables
  4. Replace the pulse coder if the fault persists with known-good cabling
