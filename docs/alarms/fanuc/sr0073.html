Fanuc Alarm SR0073 — SERIAL SPINDLE: COMMUNICATION ERROR

critical

Communication between the CNC and serial spindle amplifier has been interrupted.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SR0073

  • Serial communication cable between CNC and spindle amplifier disconnected
  • Serial communication cable damaged
  • Spindle amplifier power off
  • Serial communication parameter mismatch

How to clear alarm SR0073

  1. Check serial communication cable between NCK and spindle amplifier
  2. Verify spindle amplifier is powered on
  3. Power cycle the machine completely
  4. Check FANUC serial spindle parameters (parameter 3701-3716)
