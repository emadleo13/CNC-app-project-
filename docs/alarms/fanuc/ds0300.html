Fanuc Alarm DS0300 — APC ALARM: BATTERY LOW

warning

The backup battery for the absolute pulse coder (absolute encoder) is low. Absolute position data will be lost when battery dies.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm DS0300

  • Battery at end of service life (typically 3-5 years for AA lithium cells)
  • Battery connector loose or corroded

How to clear alarm DS0300

  1. Replace battery BEFORE powering off machine — position data is still held in capacitor for a short time
  2. Use 3.6V lithium AA batteries as specified
  3. After replacement, verify absolute position reference has not been lost
  4. Record machine zero position before changing battery as a precaution
