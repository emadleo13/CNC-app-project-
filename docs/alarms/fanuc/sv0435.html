Fanuc Alarm SV0435 — SERVO ALARM: AMPLIFIER HEAT SINK OVERHEAT

error

The servo amplifier heat sink temperature sensor detected overheating.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0435

  • Cabinet cooling fans failed or blocked
  • High ambient temperature
  • Heavy duty cycle with insufficient cooling
  • Heat sink fins clogged with dust

How to clear alarm SV0435

  1. Check all cabinet cooling fans are operating
  2. Clean heat sink fins with dry compressed air
  3. Verify ambient temperature is within specification
  4. Reduce duty cycle — allow cooling between heavy cuts
  5. Install additional cooling if consistently overheating
