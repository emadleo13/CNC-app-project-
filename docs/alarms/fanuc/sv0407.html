Fanuc Alarm SV0407 — SERVO ALARM: AXIS ERROR EXCESS

error

The servo position error exceeded the limit while the axis was in motion.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0407

  • Mechanical binding or excessive friction
  • Axis collision or crash
  • Feed rate commanded too high for servo capability
  • Servo gain too low
  • Drive motor or encoder fault

How to clear alarm SV0407

  1. Check axis for mechanical binding or collision
  2. Reduce feed rates and rapid override to 25%
  3. Verify servo gain parameters are correctly set
  4. Check encoder feedback cable
  5. Inspect for damage from crash — check motor coupling
