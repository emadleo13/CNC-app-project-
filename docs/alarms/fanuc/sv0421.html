Fanuc Alarm SV0421 — SERVO ALARM: EXCESS ERROR (STOP)

error

The axis following error exceeded the limit while the axis was at rest.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0421

  • External force displacing axis
  • Brake failure
  • Low holding torque at standstill
  • Controller tuning issue

How to clear alarm SV0421

  1. Remove any external forces on the axis
  2. Check brake operation
  3. Adjust servo parameters for better position holding
  4. Check for vibration or resonance in the axis
