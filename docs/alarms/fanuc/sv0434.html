Fanuc Alarm SV0434 — SERVO ALARM: AMPLIFIER OVERCURRENT

critical

The servo amplifier output stage detected an overcurrent in the motor output phases.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0434

  • Motor phase short circuit
  • Motor cable insulation failure
  • Amplifier output stage failure
  • Incorrect motor parameter causing overcurrent

How to clear alarm SV0434

  1. Disconnect motor cable and check motor insulation (megohmmeter test)
  2. Check motor cable for damage
  3. Verify motor rated current matches amplifier parameter settings
  4. If motor and cable are good, amplifier power module may be faulty
