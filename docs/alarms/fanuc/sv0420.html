Fanuc Alarm SV0420 — SERVO ALARM: EXCESS ERROR (MOTION)

error

The axis following error exceeded the limit during movement. The motor cannot keep up with the commanded move.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0420

  • Programmed feed rate too high for the axis
  • Mechanical overload (crash, binding, excessive friction)
  • Motor or drive torque limit too low
  • FSSB bus fault causing position command loss

How to clear alarm SV0420

  1. Reduce feed rate and rapid override
  2. Check for mechanical binding or collision damage
  3. Verify axis maximum velocity parameter (1422)
  4. Check FSSB fiber optic cable for damage
