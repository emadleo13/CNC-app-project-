Fanuc Alarm OH0700 — CONTROL UNIT OVERHEAT

critical

The CNC control unit (operator panel / CNC board) temperature has exceeded the maximum operating temperature.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm OH0700

  • Control panel cooling fans failed
  • Ambient temperature too high in control room
  • Blocked ventilation in operator panel
  • Fan filter clogged

How to clear alarm OH0700

  1. Check cooling fans in the CNC operator panel
  2. Clean fan filters on the operator panel
  3. Verify control room ambient temperature is within spec (< 40 C)
  4. Allow CNC to cool before restarting
