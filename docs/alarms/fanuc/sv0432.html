Fanuc Alarm SV0432 — SERVO ALARM: CONVERTER LOW VOLTAGE

critical

The DC bus voltage in the servo amplifier dropped below the minimum operating level.

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0432

  • Input three-phase supply voltage low or phase missing
  • Regeneration resistor fault
  • High load causing voltage droop
  • Power supply fault

How to clear alarm SV0432

  1. Check three-phase input voltage — must be within spec (typically 200V or 400V ±10%)
  2. Verify all three phases are present — check for blown fuse
  3. Check regeneration resistor connection and condition
  4. Measure DC bus voltage during operation if possible
