Fanuc Alarm SV0410 — SERVO ALARM: AXIS STOP ERROR

error

The axis position error exceeded the limit while the axis was at rest (stopped).

Likely causes of Fanuc alarm SV0410

  • External force pushing axis from commanded position
  • Brake not holding axis in position
  • Servo gain too low
  • Axis holding torque insufficient
  • Axis servo loop not tuned correctly

How to clear alarm SV0410

  1. Check if anything is pushing against the axis when stopped
  2. Verify axis brake is operating (if equipped)
  3. Increase servo position gain (parameter 1825)
  4. Check for mechanical play in ballscrew or coupling
