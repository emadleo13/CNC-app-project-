Haas Alarm 106 — MOTOR SHORT

critical

A motor phase-to-phase short circuit has been detected by the servo drive. The drive has shut down to protect the motor and power stage.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 106

  • Motor winding insulation breakdown
  • Motor cable insulation damaged by chafing or coolant
  • Connector pins shorted by contamination
  • Motor winding burned out from overload

How to clear alarm 106

  1. Disconnect motor cable and measure motor winding insulation resistance (must be > 1 MOhm)
  2. Measure phase-to-phase resistance — all three phases should be balanced
  3. Inspect motor cable for damage, especially at flexing points
  4. Check connector for coolant or metal chip contamination
  5. Replace motor if windings are shorted
