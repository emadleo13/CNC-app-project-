Haas Alarm 111 — SERVO DRIVE DISCONNECT

critical

Communication between the CNC and servo drive was lost.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 111

  • Loose or damaged servo drive communication cable
  • Drive hardware failure
  • Power supply issue to the drive

How to clear alarm 111

  1. Check servo drive cable connections at both the drive and the MOCON/IOPCB
  2. Power cycle the machine fully (main breaker off, wait 30s, on)
  3. Inspect cables for pinching or damage
