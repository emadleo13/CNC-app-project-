Haas Alarm 133 — PROBE NOT TRIPPED

warning

The touch probe did not trip (make contact) during a protected move (G31). The probe reached the end of the programmed move without triggering.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 133

  • Probe approach distance too short — target surface not reached
  • Probe stylus bent or broken and cannot reach surface
  • Workpiece not in expected location
  • Probe not functioning (battery dead, signal not transmitting)
  • Wrong axis direction programmed for approach

How to clear alarm 133

  1. Increase the probe approach distance in the G31 move
  2. Verify workpiece is in the expected position
  3. Check probe function by manually deflecting and verifying signal
  4. Inspect probe stylus for damage or contamination
  5. Verify probe feed direction matches the approach to the surface
