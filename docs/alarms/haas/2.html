Haas Alarm 2 — TOOL CHANGE FAULT

error

The automatic tool changer (ATC) did not complete the tool change sequence within the expected time or encountered an error.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 2

  • ATC arm or carousel jammed by debris or oversized tool
  • ATC sensor malfunction or misalignment
  • Low air pressure preventing pneumatic actuators from completing
  • Tool not properly seated in spindle or pocket
  • ATC motor or drive fault

How to clear alarm 2

  1. Check air pressure is above 90 PSI / 6.2 bar
  2. Follow the Haas ATC recovery procedure carefully
  3. Inspect ATC carousel and arm for debris or jammed tools
  4. Verify all ATC sensor signals in Diagnostics > Inputs
  5. Do NOT manually force the ATC mechanism
