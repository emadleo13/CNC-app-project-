Haas Alarm 8 — HIGH SPINDLE LOAD

warning

The spindle load exceeded the safety limit threshold. This may indicate a dull tool, excessive cutting parameters, or material harder than expected.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 8

  • Dull or broken cutting tool
  • Feed rate or depth of cut too aggressive
  • Material harder than programmed
  • Chip packing in the cut reducing chip evacuation
  • Tool deflection causing increased load

How to clear alarm 8

  1. Inspect and replace the cutting tool if worn or broken
  2. Reduce feed rate using feed override during cutting
  3. Reduce depth of cut or step-over in CAM program
  4. Improve chip evacuation with coolant and air blast
  5. Verify material type matches programmed expectations
