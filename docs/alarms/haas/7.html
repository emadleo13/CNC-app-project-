Haas Alarm 7 — LOW LUBE LEVEL

warning

The automatic lubrication system oil level is low. Continued operation without adequate lubrication will damage guides and ballscrews.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 7

  • Lube oil reservoir running low from normal consumption
  • Lube oil leak in distribution lines
  • Lube level sensor malfunction
  • Lube pump consuming more oil than expected due to worn seals

How to clear alarm 7

  1. Refill lube oil reservoir with recommended way oil (ISO VG 32 or VG 68)
  2. Inspect lube distribution lines for leaks
  3. Check lube level sensor float switch operation
  4. Do not ignore this alarm — running dry causes expensive damage to linear guides and ballscrews
