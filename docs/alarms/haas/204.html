Haas Alarm 204 — ATC ARM FAULT

critical

The automatic tool changer arm encountered a fault during tool change.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 204

  • Heavy or oversized tool jammed the ATC
  • ATC arm sensor failure
  • Hydraulic or pneumatic pressure loss
  • Tool not fully seated in pocket

How to clear alarm 204

  1. Do NOT force the ATC manually — follow the ATC recovery procedure
  2. Press ZERO RET to run ATC recovery sequence
  3. Check air pressure supply (minimum 90 PSI / 6.2 bar)
  4. Inspect ATC arm sensor signals in Diagnostics
  5. Ensure all tools are properly seated and within weight limits
