Haas Alarm 143 — AXIS DRIVE NOT READY

error

One or more axis drives are not reporting a ready status. The CNC cannot command motion until all drives are ready.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 143

  • Drive still initializing after power-up
  • Drive fault preventing ready state
  • Drive enable signal missing from PLC/safety chain
  • Drive power supply not at operating level
  • Communication fault between CNC and drive

How to clear alarm 143

  1. Wait 30 seconds after power-up for drives to initialize
  2. Check servo drive LEDs for fault indicators
  3. Verify E-Stop chain is satisfied and safety relay is energized
  4. Check drive power supply voltage
  5. Power cycle the machine if drives do not become ready after 60 seconds
