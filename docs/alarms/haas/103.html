Haas Alarm 103 — AXIS NOT HOMED

warning

A program or function was requested that requires the machine to be homed first.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 103

  • Machine was powered on but axes were not homed
  • Homing sequence was interrupted
  • E-Stop or alarm occurred during homing

How to clear alarm 103

  1. Press POWER/RESTART, then press ZERO RET to home all axes
  2. If home switch fault, check Parameters 22 and 1 for home direction/offset
  3. Verify home switch signals in Diagnostics > Inputs
