Haas Alarm 210 — LOW LUBE FAULT

error

The automatic lubrication system is low on lubricant or has a fault.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 210

  • Lube reservoir is empty
  • Lube pump fault
  • Lube line blockage

How to clear alarm 210

  1. Check the lube reservoir level and refill with recommended way oil (ISO VG 32)
  2. Prime the lube pump manually using the purge button
  3. Inspect lube lines for kinks or blockages
  4. Check the lube pump output pressure
  5. Do not run machine long without lubrication — causes guide and screw damage
