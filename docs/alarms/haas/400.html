Haas Alarm 400 — APC FAULT

error

The Automatic Pallet Changer sequence did not complete successfully. The pallet exchange operation was interrupted or failed.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 400

  • Pallet not properly seated on locating pins
  • Pallet clamp sensors not detecting locked state
  • Hydraulic pressure insufficient for pallet clamping
  • APC mechanism jammed or obstructed
  • Safety interlock preventing pallet exchange

How to clear alarm 400

  1. Check pallet seating and clamp indicators
  2. Verify hydraulic pressure is at operating level
  3. Inspect APC mechanism for obstructions or debris
  4. Check APC sensor signals in Diagnostics
  5. Follow APC recovery procedure in machine manual
