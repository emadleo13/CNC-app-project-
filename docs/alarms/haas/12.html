Haas Alarm 12 — OVERTRAVEL Z

error

The Z axis has reached its travel limit switch. Motion in the overtravel direction is blocked.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 12

  • Program commanded Z position outside machine travel
  • Tool length offset not active causing Z to travel too far
  • Incorrect work offset (G54-G59) Z value
  • Limit switch wiring or hardware fault

How to clear alarm 12

  1. Jog Z axis away from the limit switch in the opposite direction
  2. Ensure G43 H_ tool length compensation is active before Z cutting moves
  3. Verify work offset Z coordinate and tool length offset values
  4. Test limit switch signal in Diagnostics > Inputs
