Haas Alarm 190 — DOOR OPEN DURING CYCLE

error

The machine door interlock detected the door is open while a cycle is running.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 190

  • Safety door opened by operator during cycle
  • Door latch or interlock switch malfunction
  • Door switch signal intermittent

How to clear alarm 190

  1. Close the safety door completely and press CYCLE START
  2. Check door latch mechanism for proper engagement
  3. Test door switch signal in Diagnostics > Inputs
  4. Do NOT bypass the interlock — safety hazard
