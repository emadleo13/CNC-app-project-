Haas Alarm 311 — AIR PRESSURE LOW

warning

Machine air supply pressure dropped below minimum threshold during operation.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 311

  • Air compressor can't keep up with demand
  • Air filter regulator clogged
  • Pneumatic leak in machine
  • Compressor duty cycle exceeded

How to clear alarm 311

  1. Check air supply pressure at machine regulator (should be 90-100 PSI)
  2. Drain and clean the air filter/regulator
  3. Inspect pneumatic lines for leaks (use soap solution)
  4. Ensure compressor is large enough for machine requirements
