Haas Alarm 401 — ATC FAULT

critical

The automatic tool changer has encountered a general fault condition.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 401

  • Tool changer arm or carousel is jammed
  • Sensor detecting wrong position
  • Low air pressure
  • Tool not latched in carousel pocket

How to clear alarm 401

  1. Follow the Haas ATC Recovery Procedure (see programming manual)
  2. Check air pressure supply (must be >= 90 PSI / 6.2 bar)
  3. Inspect ATC carousel for jammed tools or debris
  4. Check all ATC sensor inputs in Diagnostics
  5. Do not manually force the ATC mechanism
