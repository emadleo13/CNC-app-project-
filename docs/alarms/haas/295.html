Haas Alarm 295 — COOLANT LEVEL LOW

warning

The coolant tank level is below the minimum sensor threshold.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 295

  • Coolant evaporated or was consumed during cutting
  • Coolant leak in system
  • Coolant drain left open

How to clear alarm 295

  1. Refill coolant tank with properly mixed coolant (check concentration)
  2. Inspect for coolant leaks around pump, hoses, and fittings
  3. Check coolant concentration with refractometer — aim for 8-10% for most materials
  4. Inspect coolant drain plug
