Haas Alarm 105 — ENCODER FAULT

critical

An axis encoder signal has been lost or corrupted. The CNC cannot determine the accurate position of the affected axis.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 105

  • Encoder cable damaged or connector loose
  • Encoder hardware failure due to coolant intrusion
  • Encoder disk contaminated or cracked
  • Electrical noise corrupting encoder signals
  • Encoder supply voltage missing

How to clear alarm 105

  1. Check encoder cable connections at both the motor and the drive/interface
  2. Inspect encoder for coolant or chip contamination
  3. Verify encoder supply voltage (typically 5V)
  4. Check cable shielding and grounding
  5. Replace encoder if signal cannot be restored
