Haas Alarm 360 — E-STOP CIRCUIT OPEN

critical

The E-Stop safety chain has an open circuit — the safety relay is not energized.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 360

  • E-Stop button pressed
  • Safety door switch open
  • Light curtain or safety mat triggered
  • Wiring fault in E-Stop circuit

How to clear alarm 360

  1. Check and release any pressed E-Stop buttons (twist clockwise)
  2. Close all safety doors
  3. Verify light curtains and safety mats are unobstructed
  4. Trace E-Stop chain with electrical drawings to find open contact
