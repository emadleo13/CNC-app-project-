Haas Alarm 171 — SPINDLE LOAD MONITOR

warning

Spindle load exceeded the programmed threshold. Cycle may have been halted.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 171

  • Dull or broken cutting tool
  • Feed rate too high for material and tool
  • Depth of cut too aggressive
  • Wrong material type entered

How to clear alarm 171

  1. Inspect and replace the cutting tool
  2. Reduce feed rate and/or depth of cut
  3. Check Material Override and Tool Load Monitor settings
  4. Verify spindle load limit Parameter (setting 84)
