Haas Alarm 101 — SERVO ERROR TOO LARGE

error

The axis position following error exceeded the maximum allowed value. The motor cannot keep up with the commanded position.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 101

  • Mechanical binding or obstruction on the axis
  • Worn or damaged ballscrew, linear guides, or bearings
  • Servo drive or motor fault
  • Excessive feed rate or acceleration settings
  • Low servo drive voltage

How to clear alarm 101

  1. Power off and check for mechanical binding — move axis by hand
  2. Inspect ballscrew, linear guides, wipers, and bearings
  3. Reduce rapid override to 25% and test — if alarm clears, check parameters
  4. Check servo drive LEDs for secondary fault codes
  5. Check Parameters 15 and 16 (following error limits)
  6. Verify correct lubrication on all axes
