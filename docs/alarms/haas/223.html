Haas Alarm 223 — SPINDLE DRIVE OVERLOAD

error

The spindle drive detected sustained overcurrent — the spindle was overloaded beyond its rated capacity.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 223

  • Cutting too aggressively in hard material
  • Dull tool requiring more force
  • Tool dig-in or crash
  • Drive current limit set too low

How to clear alarm 223

  1. Reduce spindle load — lower depth of cut, feed rate, or speed
  2. Inspect and replace cutting tool
  3. Check spindle drive current limit parameters
  4. Allow drive to cool before restarting
