Haas Alarm 375 — LUBE PRESSURE LOW

warning

The way lube system oil pressure is below the minimum required threshold.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 375

  • Way lube oil reservoir low or empty
  • Lube pump fault
  • Lube line cracked or disconnected
  • Lube pressure switch fault

How to clear alarm 375

  1. Check and refill way lube oil reservoir
  2. Test lube pump operation manually
  3. Inspect lube lines for cracks or disconnected fittings
  4. Check lube pressure switch operation
