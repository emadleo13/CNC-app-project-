Haas Alarm 112 — SPINDLE DRIVE FAULT

critical

The spindle drive hardware has experienced a failure. The spindle cannot operate until the fault is resolved.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 112

  • Spindle drive power module failure
  • Spindle drive control board fault
  • Input power fault (phase loss or voltage sag)
  • Spindle motor winding short circuit
  • Drive firmware crash

How to clear alarm 112

  1. Check spindle drive display for specific fault sub-code
  2. Power cycle the machine completely (main breaker off, wait 60s)
  3. Verify three-phase input power is balanced and within specification
  4. Measure spindle motor winding insulation resistance
  5. Contact Haas service if drive hardware is confirmed faulty
