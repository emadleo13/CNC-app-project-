Haas Alarm 306 — SPINDLE SPEED ERROR

error

The spindle could not reach or maintain the commanded speed within the allowed time.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 306

  • Spindle load too high — stalled or near stall
  • Encoder fault — spindle speed feedback incorrect
  • Drive fault limiting spindle power
  • Belt broken or slipping (belt-driven machines)

How to clear alarm 306

  1. Reduce programmed spindle speed and load
  2. Check spindle encoder signal and wiring
  3. Inspect belt condition and tension (if belt-drive model)
  4. Check spindle drive for fault codes
