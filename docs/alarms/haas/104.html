Haas Alarm 104 — SERVO COMMUNICATION

critical

Communication with a servo drive was lost. The CNC cannot send or receive position/velocity data from the affected axis drive.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 104

  • Servo communication cable disconnected or damaged
  • Servo drive powered off or not booted
  • Communication interface board fault on CNC or drive
  • Electrical noise causing communication errors
  • Drive firmware crash

How to clear alarm 104

  1. Check servo communication cable connections at both the drive and controller
  2. Power cycle the machine completely (main breaker off, wait 60s, on)
  3. Inspect communication cables for damage or pinching
  4. Verify drive power LEDs indicate normal boot sequence
  5. Check for electrical noise sources near communication cables
