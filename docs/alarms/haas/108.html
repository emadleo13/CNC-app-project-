Haas Alarm 108 — HIGH CURRENT

critical

The drive DC bus current has exceeded the maximum allowable limit. The drive has shut down to prevent damage to power electronics.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 108

  • Motor short circuit or insulation failure
  • Excessive mechanical load on axis
  • Drive power module degradation
  • Incorrect motor parameters in drive configuration
  • Sudden impact or collision on the axis

How to clear alarm 108

  1. Check motor insulation resistance with megohmmeter
  2. Inspect motor cable for damage
  3. Verify motor rated current matches drive parameter settings
  4. Check for mechanical binding or collision damage on the axis
  5. Power cycle and monitor — replace drive if fault persists with motor disconnected
