Haas Alarm 309 — CHIP CONVEYOR FAULT

warning

The chip conveyor has detected a fault — it may be jammed, overloaded, or have a motor fault.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 309

  • Large chip accumulation jamming the conveyor
  • Conveyor belt tension too tight or too loose
  • Conveyor motor overload
  • Chip type not suitable for conveyor design

How to clear alarm 309

  1. Clear chip accumulation from the conveyor manually (machine off)
  2. Check and adjust belt tension
  3. Reset conveyor motor overload (thermal reset button near motor)
  4. Break up large chips before they enter the conveyor
