Haas Alarm 109 — DRIVE OVER TEMPERATURE

error

A servo amplifier reached its thermal limit. The drive will shut down to prevent damage.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 109

  • Cabinet cooling fan failure
  • Ambient temperature too high
  • Drive has been overloaded for extended period
  • Blocked air vents in cabinet

How to clear alarm 109

  1. Allow machine to cool — wait 30-60 minutes before restart
  2. Check all cabinet cooling fans are spinning correctly
  3. Clean dust from drive heat sinks and cabinet vents
  4. Verify ambient temperature is within specification (< 40 C)
  5. Reduce duty cycle or cutting loads
