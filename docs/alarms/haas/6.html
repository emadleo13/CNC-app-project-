Haas Alarm 6 — SPINDLE FAULT

error

The spindle drive has reported an error condition. The spindle cannot operate until the fault is cleared.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 6

  • Spindle drive internal hardware fault
  • Spindle motor overload or overcurrent
  • Spindle encoder signal lost
  • Power supply voltage fluctuation
  • Spindle bearing seizure

How to clear alarm 6

  1. Check spindle drive display for specific fault sub-code
  2. Power cycle the machine completely (main breaker off, wait 60s)
  3. Verify three-phase power supply is balanced and stable
  4. Inspect spindle motor connections and encoder cable
  5. Check spindle for unusual noise or vibration before restarting
