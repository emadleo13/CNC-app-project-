Haas Alarm 202 — SPINDLE FEEDBACK FAULT

error

The spindle tachometer or encoder feedback signal is missing or out of range.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 202

  • Spindle encoder cable disconnected or damaged
  • Encoder hardware failure
  • Electrical noise on encoder cable
  • Spindle drive parameter mismatch

How to clear alarm 202

  1. Check spindle encoder cable at the encoder and drive connection
  2. Inspect cable for damage or pinching
  3. Verify encoder supply voltage
  4. Power cycle and monitor — intermittent encoder issues may recur
  5. Replace encoder if signal is consistently missing
