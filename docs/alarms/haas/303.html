Haas Alarm 303 — SPINDLE OVERLOAD

error

The spindle motor load exceeded the overload threshold.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 303

  • Dull or broken cutting tool
  • Feed rate or depth of cut too aggressive
  • Wrong material — harder than expected
  • Chip packing in the cut

How to clear alarm 303

  1. Inspect and replace cutting tool
  2. Reduce feed rate using feed override
  3. Reduce depth of cut or step-over
  4. Improve chip evacuation with coolant and air blast
  5. Check spindle load limit setting (Setting 84)
