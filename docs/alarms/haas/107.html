Haas Alarm 107 — AXIS MOTOR OVERLOAD

error

The axis motor current draw exceeded the overload limit. Thermal or current overload protection activated.

Likely causes of Haas alarm 107

  • Mechanical binding or excessive friction
  • Worn ballscrew or bearings requiring more force
  • Crash or tool contact with fixture
  • Incorrect axis tuning parameters

How to clear alarm 107

  1. Power off and check for mechanical obstruction on the affected axis
  2. Lubricate ballscrews and linear guides
  3. Allow machine to cool for 30 minutes before restarting
  4. Check for loose motor coupling
  5. Inspect and replace worn ballscrew/bearings if needed
