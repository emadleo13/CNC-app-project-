Heidenhain Alarm 900 — OSCILLATION DETECTED

warning

Vibration or oscillation has been detected on an axis servo. The servo system is unstable and may cause surface quality issues or mechanical damage.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 900

  • Servo gain (KV) set too high causing instability
  • Mechanical resonance in the axis drive train
  • Worn ballscrew or coupling causing backlash oscillation
  • Encoder signal quality degraded causing control loop instability
  • Loose mechanical connection in axis

How to clear alarm 900

  1. Reduce servo gain (KV factor) for the affected axis
  2. Check for mechanical looseness in ballscrew coupling, bearings, or mounts
  3. Inspect encoder signal quality
  4. Tighten all mechanical connections on the affected axis
  5. Contact machine builder for servo optimization if reducing gain does not help
