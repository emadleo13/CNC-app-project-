Heidenhain Alarm 186 — STYLUS DEFLECTION

warning

The touch probe stylus is deflected (probe triggered) at the start of a motion move. The probe must be undeflected before motion.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 186

  • Probe tip touching workpiece or fixture at program start
  • Probe still in contact from previous measurement
  • Probe stylus bent or damaged
  • Probe positioned incorrectly

How to clear alarm 186

  1. Move probe clear of all objects before starting a new motion
  2. Check probe stylus for damage or contamination
  3. Verify probe is correctly positioned before executing a measuring cycle
  4. Check probe signal state — LED on probe should be off (not triggered)
