Heidenhain Alarm 800 — ENCODER BATTERY LOW

warning

The absolute encoder backup battery voltage is low. If the battery fails, absolute position data will be lost requiring re-referencing.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 800

  • Battery reaching end of service life (typical 3-5 years)
  • Battery connector corroded or loose
  • Higher than normal battery drain

How to clear alarm 800

  1. Replace encoder battery as soon as possible — do NOT power off first
  2. Use the correct battery type as specified in machine documentation
  3. After replacement, verify all axis positions are correct
  4. If battery was completely dead, perform reference run on all axes after replacement
