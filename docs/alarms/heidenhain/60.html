Heidenhain Alarm 60 — FOLLOWING ERROR TOO LARGE

error

The difference between the commanded and actual axis position has exceeded the allowed following error tolerance.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 60

  • Feed rate is too high for the axis dynamics
  • Mechanical binding or obstruction on the axis
  • Servo loop gain parameters are set too low
  • Encoder feedback signal fault

How to clear alarm 60

  1. Reduce the programmed feed rate or acceleration
  2. Check the axis for mechanical obstructions or a worn ballscrew
  3. Verify the servo loop gain parameters against the machine manual
  4. Inspect the encoder cable and feedback signal
