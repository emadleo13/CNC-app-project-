Heidenhain Alarm 30 — SPINDLE NOT READY

error

The spindle drive is reporting a not-ready status, so the spindle cannot be started.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 30

  • An active fault or alarm is present on the spindle drive
  • The spindle enable signal was not received from the PLC
  • Spindle orientation did not complete before a tool change
  • The spindle motor thermal overload has tripped

How to clear alarm 30

  1. Check the spindle drive diagnostic display for a fault code
  2. Reset the spindle drive
  3. Allow the spindle motor to cool down if it tripped on thermal overload
  4. Verify the M3/M4 command and the spindle enable wiring
