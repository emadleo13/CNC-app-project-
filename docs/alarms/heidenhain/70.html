Heidenhain Alarm 70 — TS TOUCH PROBE NOT READY

warning

The touch probe system did not respond or report a ready status when a probing cycle was called.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 70

  • Wireless probe battery is depleted
  • The probe was not switched on before the cycle
  • The infrared transmission path is misaligned or obstructed
  • The probe stylus is already deflected at the start of the cycle

How to clear alarm 70

  1. Replace or charge the touch probe battery
  2. Switch on the probe before running the probing cycle
  3. Clean and align the infrared transmission path between probe and receiver
  4. Check that the stylus is straight and not pre-deflected
