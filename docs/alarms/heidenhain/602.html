Heidenhain Alarm 602 — DRIVE OVERTEMPERATURE

error

The servo drive module heat sink temperature has exceeded the maximum limit.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 602

  • Cabinet cooling failure
  • High ambient temperature
  • Heavy continuous cycle duty
  • Dust clogging heat sink fins

How to clear alarm 602

  1. Check all cabinet cooling fans
  2. Clean heat sink fins with dry compressed air
  3. Verify ambient temperature
  4. Reduce machine duty cycle until issue is resolved
