Heidenhain Alarm 303 — ENCODER MONITORING — SIGNAL FAULT

critical

The position encoder signal has a hardware fault. The signal amplitude, frequency, or integrity check failed.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 303

  • Encoder cable damaged or connector loose
  • Encoder hardware failure
  • Electrical interference on encoder cable
  • Encoder contaminated with coolant or oil

How to clear alarm 303

  1. Check encoder cable connections at motor and at the encoder board
  2. Inspect cable for pinching or damage
  3. Verify encoder cable shielding is properly grounded
  4. Clean encoder and read head if contaminated
  5. Replace encoder if signal fault persists
