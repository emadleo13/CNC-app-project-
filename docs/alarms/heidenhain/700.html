Heidenhain Alarm 700 — TEMPERATURE WARNING

warning

A motor or drive temperature is approaching its limit. This is a pre-alarm warning before a shutdown occurs.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 700

  • Heavy cutting load sustained for extended period
  • Cooling system partially degraded
  • Ambient temperature elevated
  • Dust accumulation reducing cooling efficiency
  • Increased friction from worn bearings

How to clear alarm 700

  1. Reduce cutting load and allow temperatures to stabilize
  2. Check all cooling fans are operating at full speed
  3. Clean heat sinks and air filters
  4. Verify ambient temperature is within specification
  5. Schedule maintenance to check bearings and lubrication
