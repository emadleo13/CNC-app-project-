Heidenhain Alarm 400 — PROBE CONTACT

warning

Unexpected probe contact occurred during a rapid move. The touch probe was triggered when it should not have been.

Likely causes of Heidenhain alarm 400

  • Probe stylus hit workpiece or fixture during rapid positioning
  • Probe approach path incorrect
  • Workpiece or fixture positioned differently than expected
  • Probe signal noise causing false trigger

How to clear alarm 400

  1. Check workpiece and fixture positions — ensure clearance for probe movement
  2. Modify approach path to avoid obstacles
  3. Verify safe clearance height is sufficient
  4. Check probe signal for noise or intermittent false triggers
  5. Inspect probe stylus for bending that could cause unexpected contact
