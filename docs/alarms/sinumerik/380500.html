Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 380500 — Drive: motor overtemperature warning

warning

The motor temperature sensor reports the motor is approaching or has exceeded its thermal limit.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 380500

  • Excessive machining load
  • Motor cooling fan fault
  • Ambient temperature too high
  • Motor thermistor wiring fault

How to clear alarm 380500

  1. Allow machine to cool — reduce cycle duty or take a break
  2. Check motor cooling fan is spinning
  3. Verify ambient temperature is within spec (max 40 C)
  4. Reduce cutting load (feed, depth of cut, or speed)
  5. Check motor thermistor wiring at motor connector
