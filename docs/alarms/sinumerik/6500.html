Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 6500 — SAFETY RELAY OPEN

critical

A safety relay is in the open state, placing the machine in a safe stop condition. All drives are disabled.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 6500

  • Emergency stop button pressed
  • Safety door interlock open
  • Safety relay hardware fault
  • Safety monitoring function triggered (STO, SS1, etc.)
  • Light curtain or safety mat activated

How to clear alarm 6500

  1. Check all E-Stop buttons are released
  2. Close all safety doors and verify interlock signals
  3. Check safety relay status indicators
  4. Verify all safety inputs in PLC diagnostics
  5. Press RESET after resolving the safety condition
