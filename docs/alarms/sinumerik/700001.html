Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 700001 — PLC: Emergency stop activated

critical

The PLC has activated the emergency stop condition. All drives have been disabled.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 700001

  • E-STOP button pressed
  • Safety circuit open (door, guard, light curtain)
  • PLC program error triggering E-STOP output

How to clear alarm 700001

  1. Check and release E-STOP button (twist to release)
  2. Verify all safety doors are closed and guards are in place
  3. Check PLC diagnostics for the specific input that triggered the E-STOP
  4. Press RESET after clearing the cause
