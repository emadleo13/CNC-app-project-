Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 22020 — Position tolerance exceeded

error

The axis position error is larger than the defined tolerance. Contour monitoring detected a control deviation.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 22020

  • Following error too large due to mechanical friction
  • Servo gain (KV) set too low
  • Drive not energized properly
  • Mechanical backlash exceeding tolerance

How to clear alarm 22020

  1. Check and adjust KV (servo gain) parameters
  2. Inspect mechanical components for excessive friction or backlash
  3. Verify drive is fully enabled and receiving current
  4. Check encoder cable quality and shielding
