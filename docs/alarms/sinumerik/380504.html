Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 380504 — Drive: drive unit overtemperature

error

The drive module heat sink temperature has exceeded the limit.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 380504

  • Cabinet cooling failure
  • High ambient temperature
  • Heavy duty cycle
  • Heat sink fins clogged with dust

How to clear alarm 380504

  1. Allow drive to cool — stop machine for 30 minutes
  2. Check all cabinet fans are running
  3. Clean heat sink fins with dry compressed air
  4. Reduce duty cycle or add forced ventilation to cabinet
