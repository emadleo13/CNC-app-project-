Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 20003 — Axis move commanded while spindle stopped

warning

A feed move was commanded while the spindle was not rotating. This is blocked as a safety measure.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 20003

  • M3 or M4 not programmed before feed moves
  • Spindle failed to start
  • Spindle interlock not satisfied

How to clear alarm 20003

  1. Ensure M03 S_ (or M04 S_) is programmed before any G01/G02/G03 moves
  2. Check that the spindle has actually reached speed
  3. Verify spindle enable signal in PLC diagnostics
