Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 21612 — SYNCHRONIZATION ERROR

critical

Axis synchronization has been lost during gantry operation. The position difference between coupled axes exceeds the allowed tolerance.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 21612

  • Mechanical binding on one axis of the gantry pair
  • Encoder fault on one axis causing position mismatch
  • Different mechanical load on gantry axes
  • Servo tuning mismatch between paired axes
  • Collision or obstruction on one side of gantry

How to clear alarm 21612

  1. Check both gantry axes for mechanical binding or obstruction
  2. Verify encoder signals on both axes
  3. Check synchronization tolerance in machine data
  4. Re-home (reference) both gantry axes
  5. Inspect for collision damage on the unsynchronized axis
