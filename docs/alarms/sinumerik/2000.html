Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 2000 — Contour monitoring: velocity too high

critical

The actual axis velocity exceeded the contour monitoring tolerance band.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 2000

  • Axis runaway condition
  • Incorrect encoder polarity
  • Drive fault causing uncontrolled motion

How to clear alarm 2000

  1. Emergency stop the machine
  2. Check encoder wiring and polarity
  3. Verify drive enable chain is correct
  4. Check for additional drive alarms
