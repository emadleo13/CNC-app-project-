Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 10602 — DRIVE POWER FAILURE

critical

The power module of a drive reports a fault condition. The drive cannot deliver power to the motor.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 10602

  • Power module hardware failure
  • DC bus undervoltage or overvoltage
  • Power module overtemperature
  • Motor short circuit detected by power module
  • Input power phase loss

How to clear alarm 10602

  1. Check drive module fault LED or display for specific error
  2. Verify three-phase input power supply
  3. Check DC bus voltage level
  4. Inspect motor cable for short circuit or damage
  5. Power cycle and monitor — replace power module if fault persists
