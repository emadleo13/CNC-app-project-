Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 17630 — Subprogram nesting depth exceeded

warning

The maximum subprogram call depth (typically 8 levels) has been exceeded.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 17630

  • Recursive subprogram call (program calls itself directly or indirectly)
  • Too many levels of subroutine nesting

How to clear alarm 17630

  1. Restructure program to reduce nesting depth
  2. Check for accidental recursive calls (subprogram calls itself)
  3. Reduce number of nested CALL/MCALL levels
