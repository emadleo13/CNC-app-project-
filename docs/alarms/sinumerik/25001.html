Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 25001 — Emergency stop activated

critical

The Emergency Stop button has been pressed or the E-STOP chain has been interrupted.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 25001

  • Operator pressed the E-STOP button
  • Safety door opened during cycle
  • E-STOP relay chain fault
  • Drive safety monitoring triggered ESTOP

How to clear alarm 25001

  1. Identify and resolve the cause of the E-STOP before clearing
  2. Release the E-STOP button (twist clockwise)
  3. Press RESET on the operator panel
  4. Check the PLC diagnostics for the E-STOP chain signal
  5. Check all safety door and light curtain statuses
