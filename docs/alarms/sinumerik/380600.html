Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 380600 — Drive: overcurrent / short circuit

critical

The drive detected an overcurrent condition in the power stage or motor winding.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 380600

  • Motor winding short circuit or insulation breakdown
  • Motor phase-to-phase or phase-to-ground fault
  • Drive power stage failure
  • Cable insulation damage

How to clear alarm 380600

  1. Check motor insulation resistance with a megohmmeter (should be > 1 MOhm)
  2. Inspect motor cable for damage, chafing, or pinching
  3. Measure motor winding resistance — all phases should be balanced
  4. If motor and cables are good, suspect the drive power module
