Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 380503 — Drive: motor temperature alarm

critical

The servo motor temperature has exceeded the maximum threshold. The drive has stopped the axis to prevent motor damage.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 380503

  • Sustained heavy cutting cycles without rest
  • Motor cooling fan failure
  • Blocked motor ventilation
  • Motor bearing failure generating heat

How to clear alarm 380503

  1. Stop production and allow motor to cool for 30+ minutes
  2. Check motor cooling fan operation
  3. Clear any obstruction to motor ventilation
  4. After cooling, reduce duty cycle
  5. If alarm recurs immediately, inspect motor bearings
