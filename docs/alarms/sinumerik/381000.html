Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 381000 — Drive: encoder fault

critical

The position encoder feedback signal is missing, incorrect, or has a hardware fault.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 381000

  • Encoder cable damaged, disconnected, or with a loose connector
  • Encoder hardware failure
  • Electrical interference on encoder cable
  • Wrong encoder type configured in machine data

How to clear alarm 381000

  1. Check encoder cable connections at both the motor and the drive module
  2. Inspect cable for damage, especially at cable entry points
  3. Measure encoder supply voltage (typically 5V or 24V depending on type)
  4. Verify machine data for encoder type matches installed hardware (MD30200-MD30250)
  5. Replace encoder if all wiring checks out
