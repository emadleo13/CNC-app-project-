Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 4050 — SPINDLE ORIENTATION FAULT

error

The M19 spindle orientation command did not complete within the allowed time. The spindle could not reach the programmed angular position.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 4050

  • Spindle encoder fault preventing position feedback
  • Spindle brake not releasing properly
  • Orientation timeout too short for spindle inertia
  • Spindle drive fault during orientation
  • Spindle mechanical drag preventing rotation to position

How to clear alarm 4050

  1. Check spindle encoder signal quality and connections
  2. Verify spindle brake releases during orientation (check PLC I/O)
  3. Increase orientation timeout in machine data if heavy spindle
  4. Check spindle drive for additional alarms
  5. Retry M19 after clearing alarm — intermittent faults may clear
