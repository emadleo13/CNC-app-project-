Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 10604 — ENCODER SIGNAL FAULT

error

The encoder signal quality has fallen below the acceptable threshold. Position measurement accuracy cannot be guaranteed.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 10604

  • Encoder cable damaged or connector loose
  • Encoder contaminated with coolant or chips
  • Encoder signal amplitude degraded
  • Electrical interference on encoder cable
  • Encoder read head gap too large

How to clear alarm 10604

  1. Check encoder cable connections at motor and drive module
  2. Inspect encoder for contamination and clean if needed
  3. Verify encoder cable shielding is properly grounded
  4. Check signal amplitude using drive diagnostics
  5. Replace encoder if signal quality cannot be restored
