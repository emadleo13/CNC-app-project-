Siemens Sinumerik Alarm 700002 — PLC: Feed hold active

warning

The PLC feed hold signal is active. Axis motion and spindle operation are inhibited.

Likely causes of Siemens Sinumerik alarm 700002

  • Feed hold button pressed on operator panel
  • PLC interlock condition active (e.g., coolant door, workpiece clamp)
  • External signal holding the feed hold input

How to clear alarm 700002

  1. Check if the feed hold button on the operator panel is engaged
  2. Review PLC diagnostics for which interlock is asserting feed hold
  3. Ensure workpiece clamping, coolant doors, and other interlocks are satisfied
