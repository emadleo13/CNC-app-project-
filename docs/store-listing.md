CNC Assist is a practical toolkit for CNC machine operators, programmers, and machining students. Calculate cutting parameters, check your G-code, and get instant answers to machining questions — all in one app.

FEED & SPEED CALCULATOR
• Milling, turning, and drilling cut parameters
• RPM, feed rate, chip load, and material removal rate
• Carbide and HSS tooling, roughing and finishing
• Built-in material database with cutting-speed data
• Metric and imperial units

G-CODE ANALYZER
• Paste or load a program to detect errors and warnings
• Supports Haas, Siemens Sinumerik, and generic ISO dialects
• Automatic controller detection
• Line-by-line view with a clear summary

AI KNOWLEDGE BASE
• Ask machining questions in plain language and get clear answers
• Look up G/M-codes and common controller alarms
• Analyze a photo of a part, drawing, or alarm screen
• CNC guides and a G-code program library

MORE TOOLS

• Save your calculations and analyses for later
• Available in English, Romanian, Persian, and Arabic

From setting up a job to troubleshooting a program, CNC Assist helps with everyday shop tasks.

Some advanced AI features may require a subscription. A free tier is included.

This app is a productivity aid. Always verify cutting parameters and programs against your machine, tooling, and material before running.
