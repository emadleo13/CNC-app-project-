Privacy Policy

CNC Assist · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CNC Assist ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. CNC Assist is a tool for CNC machine operators that provides feed/speed calculators, a G-code analyzer, and an AI-powered knowledge base. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Developer: Emad Leo · Contact: hamidleo1984@gmail.com

1. Accounts

CNC Assist does not require you to register with a name, email address, or password. When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created automatically on our backend (Supabase) so that features such as usage limits and subscription status can work. This account is identified only by a random ID and is not linked to your personal identity.

3. AI processing

The knowledge base and analysis features send the content you submit, and nothing else about you, to one of these services, only to generate your result:

  • Anthropic (the Claude API), our main AI provider. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content submitted through the API is not used to train their models.
  • OpenRouter, used when Claude is unavailable. It forwards the request to a free AI model run by another company (for example Google or NVIDIA). These providers may log requests and may use them to improve their models, under their own terms; see OpenRouter's privacy policy.

Requests carry no account ID, name or contact details. Please avoid submitting confidential or personally identifying information in your questions, images, or files.

4. Service providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the app. They process data only on our behalf to deliver these features:

  • Supabase — backend, anonymous authentication, and database hosting
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI question answering and analysis
  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to — AI answers when Claude is unavailable
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases, verification and subscription status updates

5. Data retention

Your anonymous account, usage records, and subscription status are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (see below), this data is removed from our systems. Data stored locally on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account & data. This removes your account and your records from our backend and clears the app's local data on your device.
  • By email: you may also request deletion by writing to hamidleo1984@gmail.com.

7. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS. Access to backend data is restricted by row-level security so that each anonymous account can only access its own records. Server-side secrets (such as AI provider keys) are never stored in the app.

8. Children

CNC Assist is a professional tool intended for industrial use by adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
