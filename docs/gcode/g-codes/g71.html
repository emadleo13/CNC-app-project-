G71 — Stock Removal Roughing (Lathe)

G-Code · Fanuc, Generic

Lathe roughing cycle for OD/ID turning.

Syntax

G71 U_ R_  /  G71 P_ Q_ U_ W_ F_

Watch out

U=depth of cut, R=retract, W=finish allowance Z.
