G86 — Boring — Stop Spindle, Rapid Out

G-Code · Haas, Generic, Fanuc

Boring cycle: feeds to depth, stops spindle, retracts rapidly.

Syntax

G86 X_ Y_ Z_ R_ F_

Watch out

May leave a witness mark. Use G76 for fine bores.
