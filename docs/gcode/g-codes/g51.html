G51 — Scaling

G-Code · Haas, Sinumerik, Fanuc

Scales programmed coordinates by a factor relative to a specified center.

Syntax

G51 X_ Y_ Z_ P_ (Haas: P=scale factor × 1000)

Watch out

Cancel with G50 (Haas) or SCALE OFF (Sinumerik).
