G00 — Rapid Positioning

G-Code · Haas, Sinumerik, Generic, Fanuc

Moves tool at maximum rapid rate to specified position. Not for cutting.

Syntax

G00 X_ Y_ Z_

Watch out

No material removal. Confirm clearance before rapid moves.
