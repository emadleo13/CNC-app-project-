G50 — Maximum Spindle Speed Setting

Mazak Code · Mazak

Sets an upper RPM limit that the control will not exceed while G96 constant surface speed control is active, protecting the chuck and workpiece at small diameters.

Syntax

G50 S_ (max RPM)

Watch out

Required before G96 on parts with a wide diameter range.
